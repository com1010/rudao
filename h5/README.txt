RuDao H5 - Offline Book  /  儒道离线阅读版
====================================================================

WHAT THIS IS
  The complete RuDao (儒道) book - 33 chapters, bilingual - packed into one
  self-contained HTML file. No server, no account, no internet connection.

HOW TO READ OFFLINE (from this ZIP)
  1. Unzip the folder "RuDaoH5" anywhere on your phone or computer.
  2. Open "index.html" in any modern browser
     (Safari, Chrome, Edge, Firefox, WeChat built-in browser).
  3. Everything works offline: table of contents, search, bookmarks,
     font size and paper themes. Your position is remembered on that device.

  iPhone / iPad: Files app -> unzip -> tap index.html. For a proper app icon,
     open https://rudao.org/h5/ in Safari and use Share -> Add to Home Screen.
  Android: unzip with any file manager -> open index.html in Chrome, or open
     https://rudao.org/h5/ and use the menu -> "Add to Home screen".
  Desktop: unzip and double-click index.html.

HOW TO INSTALL AS AN APP (online once, offline forever)
  Open https://rudao.org/h5/ on the phone, then "Add to Home Screen".
  The reader caches itself on first visit and keeps working with no signal.

READER CONTROLS
  Top bar   : contents, bookmark, search, reading settings (Aa).
  Bottom bar: previous / next chapter.
  Swipe     : swipe left or right on the page to change chapter.
  Settings  : type size, paper / white / night mode, add-to-home-screen.

CONTENT
  Front Matter (preface, co-editors, dedication)
  Part I    The Roots 源 - 人性趋利
  Part II   Philosophy & Ethics 哲学与伦理
  Part III  Inner Sage, Outer King 内圣外王
  Part IV   Ru vs Strategists 儒家 - 兵家
  Part V    RuShang Legends 儒商传奇
  Part VI   Digital Civilization 数字文明
  Part VII  Back Matter 后文 (quotes, glossary, references, afterword)

  本文件已包含全书内容，无需网络即可阅读。
