AI, the Fifth Industrial Revolution, and the History of Artificial Intelligence

AI、第五次工业革命与人工智能发展史

From the philosophical perspective of RuDao (RuDao.org), technology is never a static end in itself; it is the ultimate vehicle for civilizational evolution. Every industrial revolution changes more than physical production tools - it fundamentally restructures the relationship between humanity, nature, and governance.

  • Steam engines amplified human muscle power.
  • Electricity expanded energy boundaries across entire societies.
  • Computers delegated complex data processing to digital circuits.
  • The Internet linked global human knowledge into a unified real-time network.
  • Generative AI and autonomous agents expand cognitive reasoning, decision-making, and human creativity.

Generative Artificial Intelligence is not merely another isolated technology; it marks an unprecedented era in civilizational governance. To judge where AI is heading, we must first map its trajectory across the historical coordinates of global productivity.

Why Define a Fifth Industrial Revolution?

While Industry 4.0 asked "How do we make machines smarter?", Industry 5.0 asks "How do humans and intelligent systems co-create sustainable civilizational value?"

  • From tools to autonomous agents. Traditional AI focused on recognition, classification, prediction, and optimization. Generative AI and autonomous agents generate new knowledge, draft code, execute multi-step workflows, and participate in complex decision-making.
  • From muscle to mind. The first four industrial revolutions altered physical labor, energy utilization, data throughput, and device connectivity. The Fifth Industrial Revolution directly engages human cognition, reasoning, and creative output.
  • The paradigm shift. Moving from "machine replacing human" to "human + AI co-creating value".
  • 5IR core pillars. Human-AI collaboration, augmentation, systemic resilience, Web3 digital trust, and sustainable stewardship.

When machines write, program, analyze, negotiate, and decide, AI ceases to be a passive tool and becomes what Dr. JiaMing Zhu defines as "The Third Existence" 第三种存在 (Human + AI + Environment). Six Phases of AI History

Phase 1 - Theoretical Genesis (1943-1956)

  • 1943: Warren McCulloch and Walter Pitts publish the mathematical model of an artificial neuron, initiating neural network theory.
  • 1950: Alan Turing publishes Computing Machinery and Intelligence, introducing the Turing Test ("Imitation Game") and posing the question: "Can machines think?"
  • 1956: John McCarthy, Marvin Minsky, Nathaniel Rochester, and Claude Shannon convene the Dartmouth Conference, coining the term Artificial Intelligence. Allen Newell and Herbert Simon demonstrate the Logic Theorist program.

Phase 2 - Symbolic Optimism and the First AI Winter (1956-1974)

  • 1957: Frank Rosenblatt invents the Perceptron.
  • 1960s: Symbolic AI dominates reasoning, machine translation, and computer vision.
  • 1969-1973: Minsky and Papert prove single-layer Perceptron limits in their book Perceptrons. Combined with the 1973 UK Lighthill Report and severe compute limits, government funding is slashed, triggering the First AI Winter.

Phase 3 - Expert Systems and the Second AI Winter (1980-1987)

  • 1980s: Rule-based Expert Systems (e.g. MYCIN, XCON) achieve commercial adoption across industrial logistics and medicine.
  • 1986: Geoffrey Hinton and colleagues popularize the Backpropagation algorithm for multi-layer neural networks.
  • 1987-1993: High maintenance costs, fragile rule bases, and the collapse of specialized Lisp hardware markets trigger the Second AI Winter.

Phase 4 - Compute Accumulation and Heuristic Milestones (1997-2006)

  • 1997: IBM's Deep Blue defeats World Chess Champion Garry Kasparov using brute-force compute and heuristic search.
  • 1990s-2000s: Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) and Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) mature.
  • 2006: Geoffrey Hinton, Yann LeCun, and Yoshua Bengio publish Deep Belief Networks, launching the modern Deep Learning era.

Phase 5 - The Deep Learning Renaissance (2012-2019)

  • 2012: AlexNet wins the ImageNet competition, proving the power of Big Data + GPUs + Deep Neural Networks.
  • 2014-2016: Invention of GANs and attention mechanisms. DeepMind's AlphaGo defeats world champion Lee Sedol using deep reinforcement learning and Monte Carlo tree search.
  • 2017-2018: Google publishes Attention Is All You Need, introducing the Transformer architecture. OpenAI introduces GPT-1, and Google releases BERT.

Phase 6 - Generative AI, Autonomous Agents, and 5IR Symbiosis (2020-Present)

  • 2020-2022: OpenAI releases GPT-3, followed by ChatGPT in November 2022, bringing generative AI to global mainstream markets.
  • 2023: GPT-4 and multi-modal models launch, establishing 2023 as the inauguration year of the Fifth Industrial Revolution in public consciousness.
  • 2024-2026+: Transition from answering questions to executing autonomous actions. Emergence of reasoning models, multi-agent swarms, embodied AI, and spatial video generation - turning AI into active digital labor within "The Third Existence".

Three Enduring Laws of AI Evolution

  • Revolutions require triadic alignment. No single paper or algorithm changes the world in isolation. Industrial revolutions occur only when algorithms, compute, and data mature simultaneously.
  • Winters stem from overpromised timelines. AI winters were not caused by fundamental technological failure, but by short-term over-promising outstripping actual hardware capacity.
  • The cognitive frontier. Deep Blue surpassed human brute-force computation; AlphaGo surpassed human game-tree intuition; generative AI and autonomous agents now collaborate directly in knowledge creation, system governance, and strategic planning.

Summary: From Machine Automation to Human-AI Symbiosis

The Fifth Industrial Revolution marks a historical shift from machine automation to human-AI symbiosis. While previous industrial cycles extended human muscle, energy networks, data processing, and physical device connections, the 5IR introduces non-human cognitive entities capable of reasoning, creation, and autonomous execution. By evaluating AI through the eight-decade alignment of algorithms, compute, and data, business leaders can move beyond short-term market hype. Framed by Dr. JiaMing Zhu's "Third Existence" and governed by RuDao relational ethics (Yi-Li 义利统一), the Fifth Industrial Revolution ensures that artificial intelligence serves as a collaborative partner for enduring human flourishing.

AI is not the end of the human story - it is the next vehicle of it.

1. The Moganshan Legacy: Pragmatic Reform and Adaptive Governance

(一)莫干山会议历史华章:从青年学者方案到国家决策

To understand Dr. JiaMing Zhu's role in shaping modern Chinese economic institutions, one must examine the historic September 1984 Moganshan Youth Economics Conference (莫干山会议). Dr. Zhu was a primary initiator, co-organizer and intellectual driving force behind the event.

Grassroots origins, official impact

Conceived by Dr. Zhu and fellow economist Huang Jiangnan to generate fresh ideas for urban economic reform, the conference followed an open, merit-based model: over 1,300 paper submissions were reviewed nationwide, and 124 delegates were selected entirely on academic merit, regardless of official rank or institutional title. Co-hosted by Economic Daily and local academic bodies, and attended by senior central government officials, it created an unprecedented direct bridge between grassroots scholarly thinking and top-level national policy-making.

The dual-track price consensus

Organized into seven working groups on urban economic restructuring, the conference's most consequential debate centered on price reform. Under the planned economy, nearly all commodity prices were set by administrative order, creating severe structural distortions, supply shortages and resource misallocation. Delegates debated two paths: immediate full liberalization, or a gradual "adjustment plus liberalization" approach.

The final consensus - the dual-track price system (价格双轨制), which retained planned prices for key industrial inputs while freeing market prices for above-quota output - became the conference's most historically influential policy output. It provided a pragmatic, low-shock pathway for China's gradual transition to a market economy.

The RuDao alignment: Yin Shi Zhi Yi (因时制宜)

The dual-track system was never a rigid textbook formula. It was a masterclass in the Confucian strategic principles of Adaptive Governance (Yin Shi Zhi Yi / 因时制宜) and Dynamic Balance (Zhong Yong / 中庸). By avoiding systemic shock while allowing market forces to grow organically, Dr. Zhu and his contemporaries demonstrated that sustainable institutional reform requires balancing continuity with innovation - a principle that remains central to RuDao thinking in the digital age.

2. The Third Existence: AI as a New Civilizational Actor

(二)从生产力跃迁到"第五现代化" - 人工智能作为"新物种"

Just as the Four Modernizations unlocked physical productivity in the 1980s, the Fifth Modernization addresses the cognitive and institutional architectures of the digital era.

Historically, human civilization operated within two domains: the physical world and human consciousness. Artificial intelligence, he argues, introduces a third dimension, forming a new ecosystem of Human + AI + Environment in which intelligent agents act as autonomous economic participants, not passive tools.

In a widely cited 2026 interview with National Business Daily, Dr. Zhu went further: AI is not simply a technology tool, but a "new species" reshaping the entire industrial structure, economic system and social organization. He observed that humanity is moving beyond Industry 4.0, through Industry 5.0 led by AI agents, and toward the beginnings of Industry 6.0. Within years, AI agents will become the primary contributors to labor productivity - and because they lack the material consumption needs of carbon-based humans, traditional consumption-based economic metrics will no longer apply.

The RuDao alignment: Harmony Without Uniformity (和而不同)

RuDao extends classical Chinese philosophy into this new paradigm. Where industrial models sought total mechanical control, the Digital Civilization Age demands Harmony Without Uniformity (He Er Bu Tong / 和而不同). This represents a modern extension of the traditional ideal of Unity of Humanity and Nature (Tian Ren He Yi / 天人合一). Where the ancient principle described harmony between humans and the natural world, the digital era expands it to Human-AI Collaboration (Ren Ji Xie Tong / 人机协同), anchored in the enduring values of benevolence (仁), righteousness (义), trustworthiness (信) and virtue (德). Technology expands capability; only ethics builds civilization.

3. Blockchain as Trust Infrastructure: Scaling the Confucian Value of Xin

(三)区块链重建信任基础结构 - 传统道德与数字制度的融合

Dr. Zhu was among the first major Chinese economists to frame blockchain not as a speculative financial instrument, but as a next-generation trust infrastructure. As early as 2010 - years before the 2017 and 2021 industry booms - he began systematic research into blockchain and the digital economy, making him one of the earliest mainstream scholars to argue that digital technology would reshape economic paradigms.

In a landmark 2020 address to the United Nations Blockchain Conference, titled Facing the Global Trust Crisis and Trust Deficit - Blockchain as Infrastructure for Rebuilding Human Trust, Dr. Zhu outlined his core thesis: the 21st century faces a deepening, systemic trust crisis across governments, markets, law, media and traditional moral frameworks. Legacy mechanisms for building trust - family networks, personal reputation, community norms, centralized institutions - are no longer sufficient for a global, digital society.

Blockchain, he argued, provides a new foundation for global-scale trust through:

  • Distributed consensus and immutable ledgers
  • Transparent, auditable transactions
  • Smart contracts that encode rules into executable code

The RuDao alignment: Xin (信) & Yi-Li Unity (义利合一)

In Confucian thought, Trustworthiness (Xin / 信) is the foundation of all enduring enterprise: "A nation cannot stand without the trust of its people (民无信不立)."

From a RuDao perspective, blockchain represents the technological embodiment of this ancient principle. Human virtue generates trust at the interpersonal level; blockchain technology scales trust across borderless global networks. Code-based smart contracts transform moral ideals into automated, audit-verifiable business logic - turning the Confucian principle of Profit Through Righteousness (Yi Yi Qu Li / 以义取利) from an ethical aspiration into an engineered default.

RuDao summarizes the distinction as:

Character creates trust. Technology scales trust.

The digital economy requires both.

4. Web3 + AI: Reconstructing Industry Through Co-opetition

(四)Web3与AI融合 - 竞合生态下的产业重构

In his 2024 keynote speech at the Inclusion Conference on the Bund (外滩大会), titled Web3 + AI: Reconstructing Future Industries, Dr. Zhu presented a detailed framework for how AI and Web3 can evolve together to unlock the real-economy potential of decentralized systems.

He observed that Web3 had long been "much talked about but little delivered," limited in real-world impact because it had drifted away from its original semantic-web roots and become overly tied to crypto speculation. The integration of AI, he argued, would pull Web3 out of this stagnation and give it genuine economic substance.

The unifying role of Token

Dr. Zhu highlighted the concept of Token as the critical bridge between AI and Web3. While AI tokens (units of language model processing) and Web3 tokens (units of on-chain value) have different origins, they will converge to create a unified tokenized economy. This convergence, he argued, will give Web3 a real value foundation, enable the measurement of digital assets, and infuse new meaning into Real World Assets (RWA).

He famously summarized the shift with the phrase: "Everything comes from Token."

New organizational forms and co-opetition

AI contributes intelligence; Web3 contributes coordination. Together they enable entirely new forms of economic organization, from Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAO) to distributed business ecosystems.

This aligns directly with the RuDao concept of Co-opetition (Jing He / 竞合). Industrial-era firms relied on hierarchy and command. Digital ecosystems rely on collaboration and networked coordination. The strategic progression runs:

Competition → Collaboration → Co-opetition → Ecosystem Governance

The strongest organization is no longer the largest - it is the one embedded in the most trusted ecosystem.

5. The End of Classical Cycles: Dynamic Balance in an AI-Driven Economy

(五)传统经济周期已荡然无存 - 智能时代的战略韧性

In his 2026 National Business Daily interview, Dr. Zhu delivered one of his most widely discussed conclusions: traditional business cycle theory has fundamentally broken down. The Kitchin cycle, Juglar cycle and Kuznets cycle - frameworks used by economists for decades - "have all vanished."

The reason, he explained, is that technological innovation has become the dominant force shaping growth, productivity and market transformation. Short boom-bust patterns of the industrial era have been replaced by faster, more continuous technology cycles. For business leaders, this means the strategic objective shifts from accurate prediction to adaptive capability.

The RuDao alignment: Adaptive governance and long-termism

This shift directly reflects two core RuDao principles:

  • Adaptive Governance (Yin Shi Zhi Yi / 因时制宜)
  • Dynamic Balance (Zhong Yong / 中庸)

The most enduring organizations are not the strongest at any given moment, but the most adaptive - while remaining anchored to timeless ethical principles. In the Fifth Modernization, RuDao-style long-termism means not riding fixed market waves, but maintaining unwavering ethical purpose while executing continuous technological adaptation.

6. Ethics at the Core: Profit Through Righteousness in the Digital Age

(六)伦理为核 - 数字时代的义利之辨

Perhaps the deepest convergence between Dr. Zhu's scholarship and RuDao lies in their shared conviction that economic prosperity cannot be separated from moral legitimacy, innovation from responsibility, or technology from governance.

In a widely noted 2021 dialogue with Peking University ethicist He Huaihong, Dr. Zhu explored the relationship between markets and morality through the work of Adam Smith. He argued that Smith's legacy has been widely misunderstood: far from advocating unrestrained laissez-faire, Smith grounded his economics in The Theory of Moral Sentiments, placing sympathy and ethical restraint at the foundation of commercial society.

Dr. Zhu emphasized that economic systems are embedded within social systems, and social systems rest on a moral foundation. There is a baseline that must not be crossed: conscience, and the principle that one's gain must not come at another's loss.

The RuDao alignment: Profit Through Righteousness (以义取利)

This echoes the classical Confucian principle of Profit Through Righteousness (Yi Yi Qu Li / 以义取利). RuDao affirms entrepreneurship, innovation, capital formation and digital transformation - but rejects the idea that efficiency alone determines value.

  • Without trust, markets grow fragile.
  • Without ethics, innovation becomes dangerous.
  • Without virtue, intelligence loses direction.

Profile: Dr. JiaMing Zhu 朱嘉明教授简介

Background

Born in Beijing in 1950, Dr. Zhu earned his Ph.D. in economics from the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences in 1987. He has held visiting scholar positions at Harvard University, MIT, the University of Manchester and Tufts University, served as an economist at the United Nations Industrial Development Organization (UNIDO), and taught at the University of Vienna and National Taiwan University.

Historical Impact & Reform Legacy

  • A defining figure of 1980s reform-era economic thought, and one of the "Four Gentlemen of Reform" whose research and policy proposals laid intellectual groundwork for China's economic restructuring.
  • Primary initiator and co-organizer of the historic 1984 Moganshan Conference, which produced the dual-track price reform framework adopted into national policy.
  • Began systematic research into blockchain and digital economy around 2010, far ahead of mainstream industry attention, and is recognized as a pioneer of digital-economy thought in China.

Current Leadership Roles

  • Honorary Chairman, Hong Kong Blockchain Association (HKBA.net)
  • Chairman of the Academic and Technical Committee, Hengqin Digital Finance Research Institute

Chapter Conclusion: The RuDao Executive Blueprint for Digital Civilization

The Four Modernizations transformed China by liberating industrial productivity. The Fifth Modernization will transform global civilization by integrating advanced intelligence with moral legitimacy.

Dr. JiaMing Zhu's 40-year intellectual journey - from price reform and structural economics to monetary history, blockchain trust systems and AI as a new civilizational actor - provides a rigorous analytical map of technological change. RuDao supplies the ethical compass to navigate it.

Together they outline a new leadership paradigm for the digital age:

  • Artificial Intelligence expands capability.
  • Blockchain expands trust.
  • Web3 expands coordination.
  • Classical virtue sustains enduring human civilization.

This is the RuDao path for the Fifth Modernization.
