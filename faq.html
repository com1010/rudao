The book

What is RuDao?

RuDao 儒道 is a bilingual book by Tony Tong 唐仪 on Confucian co-opetition - the 儒将 RuJiang path beyond The Art of War to balanced profit and enduring partnerships. It argues that trust, character and long-term relationships are strategic assets rather than soft ideals.

What does the word RuDao mean?

儒 Ru names the Confucian tradition of ritual, learning and moral cultivation. 道 Dao means the way, and also names the Daoist tradition of natural flow and non-forcing. Together they describe a method: virtue as the anchor, momentum as the sail.

What is the book about?

It argues that the durability of a business depends on character and trust rather than on strategic cleverness alone. It reads Confucian and Daoist classics alongside Chinese and Western business cases - FanLi 范蠡 and BaiGui 白圭, Li Ka-shing, Buffett's partnership discipline, Microsoft's rescue of Apple - and carries the argument into AI and Web4.

Who should read it?

Founders, executives, investors and students of business ethics, especially those working across Chinese and Western markets - 企业领袖, 跨境精英, readers who have outgrown The Art of War, and anyone trying to hold profit and principle together.

How is it different from other Confucian business books?

Most treatments present Confucianism as a set of moral injunctions. RuDao keeps the ethics but adds the operating method: how virtue shows up in capital allocation, hiring, partnerships and exits, and why trust compounds as an asset a competitor cannot copy.

How long is the book?

28 numbered sections across six chapters, framed by front matter 前文 and back matter 后文 - roughly 57,000 English words plus about 6,000 Chinese characters, in 37 tables and 92 quoted passages.

Is the book available in Chinese?

It is bilingual on the same page rather than published as two separate editions: the English text carries the Chinese terms in their original characters at first mention, and the key passages are given in Chinese alongside. That way a term can be searched and cited accurately in either language.

What editions and formats are there?

Three editions - Heritage, Scholar's and Quiet - each a different door into the same philosophy, released in hardcover, paperback, ebook and audiobook. Covers and current availability are listed on the editions section of the main site.

Where can I buy it?

Release details, buying links and sample chapters are published on RuDao.org first. If a link you expect is missing, the edition is not on sale in that channel yet.

Why does the book study Japanese business leaders?

Because the RuShang 儒商 tradition did not stop at the Chinese border. The book reads Shibusawa Eiichi, who held the Analects and the abacus in the same hand; Inamori Kazuo, whose management rested on respect for heaven and love for people; and Matsushita Konosuke, who insisted on building people before products.

Does the book cover AI and Web3?

Yes. It treats AI and blockchain as amplifiers of capability, not sources of judgement. Blockchain can prove that data existed; it cannot create trust. AI can optimise a decision; it cannot choose which values the optimisation serves. Trust becomes scarcer, and therefore more valuable, as technology gets cheaper.

The ideas

What is a RuShang 儒商?

A Confucian merchant - a businessperson who treats profit and principle as compatible. The type is traced to Confucius's disciple Zigong 子贡, and later to FanLi 范蠡 and BaiGui 白圭, carried into the Ming and Qing by Huizhou and Shanxi merchants. A RuShang measures success by what the enterprise contributes, not only by what it extracts. Full definition in the study guide.

What is a RuJiang 儒将?

The Confucian general - the outward-facing counterpart of the RuShang. A leader who competes in markets while remaining governed by virtue, executing with discipline, timing and restraint rather than aggression. The tradition is that of the scholar-general who would rather win a durable position than a decisive but costly victory.

What is the difference between RuShang and RuJiang?

儒商 RuShang is about the merchant's conduct: what you will and will not do for profit. 儒将 RuJiang is about the leader's execution: how you compete once the terms are set. One governs the deal, the other governs the contest - and a complete leader needs both.

What are the Eight Virtues of the RuShang?

儒商八德 are 仁 benevolence, 义 righteousness, 礼 propriety, 智 wisdom, 信 trustworthiness, 忠 loyalty, 孝 filial responsibility and 恕 reciprocity. The list adapts classical virtues to commercial life: trustworthiness governs contracts, righteousness governs which deals you refuse, reciprocity governs how you treat counterparties.

Is RuDao the same as The Art of War?

No. The Art of War 孙子兵法 is a manual for winning contests; RuDao treats it as incomplete. SunTzu 孙子 teaches speed, deception and terrain, but says little about what victory is for. Strategy without virtue produces wins that do not last, so RuDao adds trust, reputation and co-opetition to the calculation. See the side-by-side comparison.

Why combine Confucianism and Daoism?

Confucianism supplies the ethical centre: who you should be and how you should treat people. Daoism supplies the method for change: timing, non-forcing, and yielding where resistance is wasted. Alone, 儒 becomes rigid; alone, 道 becomes opportunistic. Together they give a leader both compass and sail.

What is co-opetition 竞合?

Competing where necessary and collaborating where possible inside the same relationship. The term is usually attributed to Brandenburger and Nalebuff; RuDao grounds it in the Confucian idea that lasting advantage comes from relationships that outlast a single transaction.

What does inner sage, outer king 内圣外王 mean?

Cultivate yourself first, then govern. The phrase runs from the Zhuangzi through the Great Learning 大学: investigate things, attain knowledge, make the intention sincere, rectify the mind - then order the family, the firm, the state. RuDao maps it onto personal discipline first, institutional leadership second.

What does the unity of righteousness and profit 义利合一 mean?

That ethical conduct and commercial success are not traded off against each other. Righteousness sets the boundary of what you will do for profit; inside that boundary profit is legitimate and necessary, because a firm that cannot sustain itself cannot be of service.

Does RuDao treat profit as something bad?

No. It rejects the split between virtue and profit altogether. Profit is the condition of service: a firm that cannot pay its people cannot keep its promises. What it rejects is profit extracted by breaking trust, because that gain is borrowed against the future.

What does knowledge and action as one 知行合一 mean?

Wang Yangming's formulation: understanding that does not change behaviour is not understanding. RuDao adopts it as the test for the whole framework, because a philosophy of business is proven in decisions, not in reading.

Is RuDao a religious book?

No. It treats Confucianism and Daoism as practical philosophy - accounts of how to act, not doctrines to believe. Ritual 礼 appears as an instrument of coordination and respect, not as worship; nothing in the argument requires a religious commitment.

What are the Five Constants 五常?

仁 benevolence, 义 righteousness, 礼 propriety, 智 wisdom, 信 trustworthiness. In market competition they read as: care for the counterparty, refuse the wrong deal, keep the forms that make dealing predictable, judge before acting, and do what you said. Full answer in the study guide.

The author

Author of RuDao, cross-border entrepreneur, investor and business strategist.
